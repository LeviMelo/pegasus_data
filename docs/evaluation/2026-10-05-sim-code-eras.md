# SIM and SINASC: the 1996 kit change was labelled with the wrong era's codes (2026-10-05)

**Trigger.** The Y35 Goias investigation (pegasus_project `1dccdc8`) found `translate` swapping CIRCOBITO 1/3 and LOCOCOR 2/4.

**Verified against the source.** Layout `sources/sim2025.txt` (Estrutura do SIM, CGIAE/DASNT/SVS, 07/2025): CIRCOBITO 1 accident, 2 suicide, 3 homicide; LOCOCOR 1 hospital, 2 other health establishment, 3 home, 4 street, 5 other, 6 indigenous village. The records agree: CIRCOBITO 3 holds 104 of 106 deaths with X85-Y09 in Acre 1996 and 106/106 in 2000, code 1 the V01-X59 accidents; in RJ 1990 and 1995 TIPOVIOL 1 holds all E96x (homicide) and 3 all E8-E9 accidents, and LOCOCOR 2 is where the homicides are (4,451 of 7,099, "via publica"). Before the fix `translate(system="SIM", year=2022)` returned 1 Homicidio, 3 Acidente; 2 Via publica, 4 Outro, 5 Ignorado.

**Root cause.** Two kits of one system hold same-named `.CNV` files with different codes (SIM `CID9/TAB` and `CID10/TAB`; SINASC `ANT`/`1994_1995` and `NOV`/`1996_`). `kit_validity` read a window only from a `YYYYMM-YYYYMM` filename, these kits name their era by directory, so both wrote `(system, codelist, code, valid_from NULL)`. The kit ingested later replaced the other at equal authority: path order puts `CID9` after `CID10`, so SIM's 1979-95 vocabulary served every year (28 years of data wrong), and `NOV` after `ANT`, so SINASC's 1996-on vocabulary served 1994-95. The collision was recorded (6,862 SIM conflicts) and nobody read it.

**Extent (kit comparison, label normalised for accents and punctuation).** 35 same-named codelists differ between the two eras: SIM 21, SINASC 14 (the same set the dictionary's conflict table holds for the pairs). Different **meaning**, not wording: SIM TIPOVIOL/CIRCOBITO (1 homicide vs 1 accident), LOCOCOR (2 street vs 2 other health establishment; 4, 5), ESTCIV 5 (Outro vs Uniao estavel), GRAVIDEZ 3-4, INSTRUC (school levels vs years of study), SEMANAS (cut at 20 vs 22 weeks), PARTO, IDADEMAE 66+ (61e+ vs Ign), NUMFILH 16+; SINASC RACACOR (1 Amarela vs Branca; 0 to 5 shifted), GESTACAO (0-21 vs menos de 22; 28-36 vs 28-31), PRENATAL (until 6 vs 1-3), GRAVIDEZ, INSTRUC, PARTO, PAISES (2 codes). The rest is a band or abbreviation the other kit lacks (SIM age bands FXIDAD5, FXIDAD9, FXIDADP, FXIDAWHO, IDADE, FXINFAN1, FXINFAN2: "Ign" where the 1996 kit defines the band) or a spelling (ANO, MESES, ASSMEDIC, ATESTANT, FONTINFO, SINASC APGAR, SEXO, LOCOCOR, PESO).

**Other systems with several kits** (SIH, SIA, CMD, SINAN, CNES, SISCAN, CIHA), compared the same way: SIH and SIA kits already carry windows in their names (`TAB_SIH_199201-199712`, ...), and the two copies of the same window and the CMD copies differ in spelling only. SINAN NET against ONLINE: spelling, plus junk in the ONLINE labels ("Descartado 5 ,"). Not an era split, left open (OPEN_QUESTIONS 70): SISCAN's three kits (SISCOLO4, SISMAMA, TAB_SISCAN) share one system key and differ in age bands (FETARIA, IDADE: "ate 11" against "ate 10 anos"); CNES `VersoesAntigas` (2014-16) are ingested after the current kit and can overwrite its geography and registry names.

**Fix.** ADR-0152: `tabkit._KIT_ERAS` bounds the superseded kits (SIM `CID9` 197901-199512; SINASC `ANT` and `1994_1995` 199401-199512), the current kit stays unwindowed. Kits re-read (`semantics --system SIM`, then SINASC), pack and seed rebuilt. **The first rebuild windowed the whole kit and lost current geography:** the 1994-95 SINASC kit and the CID9 kit also ship `BR_MUNICIP`, `BR_REGSAUD`, the state `*_MUNICIP` lists and other names no current kit of the system carries, so at 2015 the pack lost 30,861 shared runs (SIM and SINASC municipality labels among them) and every other system gained a per-system copy of what had been shared. Not committed. The rule became: the era binds a codelist only where a current source holds the same name for the system (`settle_era_windows`): SIM 36 codebooks and SINASC 15 stay bounded, 432,195 rows (geography, ICD-9 lists, names only the old kit has) reopened. Against the shipped pack at 2015: 250 rows in 43 small codebooks gone (codes only the 1979-95 coding defines, e.g. ANO two-digit years, FXINFAN bands), no geography or registry codelist lost; 49 labels changed, 3 in meaning (SIM TIPOVIOL/CIRCOBITO 3 Homicidio, LOCOCOR 4 Via Publica, ESTCIV 5 Uniao estavel), the rest abbreviations. Fresh-home `translate`: SIM 1990 and 1995 CIRCOBITO 1 Homicidio, LOCOCOR 2 Via publica; 1996 and 2015 1 Acidente, 2 Outro Estab de Saude, 4 Via Publica; CODMUNRES labelled in every year; SINASC 1995 `RACCOR` (the 1994-95 field) 1 Amarela, 5 Preta. **Downstream:** the `death.place` role (roles.yml, used by linkage) matched LOCOCOR labels exactly, so before the fix SIM 1996+ code 2 (other health establishment) counted as street; with the corrected labels codes 2 and 6 and the 1979-95 code 4 ('Outro') matched nothing. Variants added ('outro estab de saúde', 'aldeia indigena', 'outro'); every code of both eras now lands in its category.

**Not fixed.** In 1996-98 the schema decides, not the year: `DOFET96` and `DOFET98` carry TIPOVIOL (and the old LOCOCOR) while `DOFET97`, `DOFET99` and `DORES` carry CIRCOBITO with the new codes, so a year window cannot separate them; those two foetal files read the new labels (OPEN_QUESTIONS 70). SINASC LOCNASC 5 "Aldeia indigena" is already served by the curated 2019 layout codes (checked after the rebuild); the kit's LOCOCOR.CNV (bound by the 1994-95 `LOCAL_OCOR` field) labels 5-9 Ignorado, which is right for that layout.

**Regime.** Maintainer catalog `pegasus_data_home/`, branch `pegasus-core-fixes`, 2026-10-05; live downloads DORRJ90/95, DOAC1996/2000, DOFET90/96-99 (scratch, not kept); fresh-home `translate` checks below.
