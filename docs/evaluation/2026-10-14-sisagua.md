# SISAGUA as a time-varying context by municipality and month (BACKLOG 5.5)

**Regime.** 2026-10-14/15, this branch (`pegasus-core-fixes` 32d7902 and the ADR-0192 commit), a fresh data home, the live Portal
de Dados Abertos. `sisagua_surveillance_basic` landed for 2018 and 2025 (332 MB of zips), its 19 fields derived, then read through
`pegasus_data.fields.load_field`. The shared lake (`pegasus_core_data`, built 2026-10-11) was only read.

**What existed.** SISAGUA was already integrated by ADR-0162: 7 families, the Portal's yearly zips 2014-2026 (surveillance basic
34,195,729 rows, control basic 118,949,333, other parameters, cyanobacteria, outside the standard), 24 declared context fields
(the shares with their `_assessed` populations), all built in the shared root. What was wrong was the month.

**The defect (shared lake, read-only).** `NU_MES` is filed `01`..`09` in the surveillance files and the period derivation
accepted `1`..`9`: 7,895,234 of 34,195,729 surveillance basic rows had a month (23 %); 2018: 707,319 of 2,674,342. The month fields
had one null-month row per municipality and year holding January-September. Santa Maria 2018 had rows for months 10, 11, 12 and a
null-month row of 341 of its 534 E. coli analyses. The numerators of the shares lacked their zeros: 2018, 4,997 municipalities with
assessed E. coli samples, 3,525 with a numerator row. SNGPC has the same padding (121.0 M of 528.5 M rows with a month).

**After (ADR-0192).** Each unit equals its file's lines less the header; every row has a month; numerator and denominator have the same keys.

October-December (the outbreak's months) are at the year's level on E. coli (4.7 %, 1.8 %, 3.8 % against 3.7 %); this is not read as
evidence about the outbreak (the surveillance samples the distribution network, and E. coli is not the toxoplasma oocyst's
indicator). Santa Maria 2025: 1,384 assessed, 66 present (4.8 %), every month sampled. Rio Grande do Sul 2018: 491 municipalities,
5,150 municipality-months, 55,824 E. coli analyses.

**A year-grain reader.** `pegasus_core.gateway.context_field` selects `(u, year, value)` without `month` and `_context_counts` keeps
the last row per place, so a month-grain context entered as a year is one month of it. Before this commit its numerator also lacked
the places with no positive sample (1,472 of 4,997 in 2018). It needs to sum the months of the year (`law: sum`, now declared) or read
them as a series. Relations that took SISAGUA through it (BACKLOG 3.5's controls on E. coli and diarrhoea admissions) are to be
re-run once it does.

**Not done.** Control basic/other/outside-the-standard and cyanobacteria were not landed again (their `NU_MES` is not padded; control
fields hold months 1-12 in the shared lake). SNGPC was not re-landed (hours). The cadastros (population supplied, treatment,
catchments, carro-pipa), the semi-annual control and the sampling plan are not ingested (`DATA_SOURCES.md` §13).
