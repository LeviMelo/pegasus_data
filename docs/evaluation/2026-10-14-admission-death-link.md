# Admissions to the death certificate of any cause: a cohort link declared once, run per codes (ADR-0192)

Regime: branch `worktree-agent-a0ea7b3fb98fcb091` (from `32d7902`, `pegasus-core-fixes`), 2026-10-14. Read-only on `pegasus_core_data`: its SIH-RD 2018-2022 and SIM-DO 2018-2023 role tables were copied, and its `SIHSUS`, `SIM` and `SINAN` lake directories joined (directory junctions), into a scratch home inside the worktree (`scratch/home`, `PEGASUS_DATA_HOME`), where every run and store was written. Throwaway probes (scratchpad); artifacts `data/probes/linkage/2026-10-14-admission-death-AL-I21I22.json` and `...-other-topics.json`. Machine shared with other sessions: times vary up to 3x.

**Counted, and why it matters.** Alagoas (hospital in AL), principal diagnosis I21-I22, 2018-2022, against SIM-DO 2018-2023 of the nation (a resident of Sergipe who died in an Alagoas hospital has her certificate in Sergipe's file). The in-hospital deaths (MORTE = 1) are the deaths the link must find, known from the admission itself; the other deaths have no second source, so their false-match rate is the control's (a birth date moved by a week, whose every pair is a coincidence), checked against a field no key uses: the certificate's cause.

**The cohort.** 4,671 admissions (679 with MORTE = 1, 14.5%) of 3,879 persons, a person being a birth date, sex and residence (3,627 by birth date and sex alone: of the 261 pairs of persons sharing both, 232 are expected by chance within a birth year and sex, so residence tells namesakes apart and splits perhaps thirty people who moved); one record each, the first admission of the period. In-hospital case fatality of the cohort: 617 of 3,879 first admissions, 15.9%.

All 3,879 are followed a year (SIM ends 2023-12-31). Plausibility: in-hospital 15.9% and 30-day 17.1% sit in the 15-20% expected after an AMI admission in SUS. **Sensitivity on the deaths it must find:** 548 of the 617 in-hospital first admissions are linked (88.8%); 513 of them on the discharge day (93.6%), 99.6% with a certificate of a death in hospital and 99.5% in the hospital's municipality (held out). Independent: the national probabilistic `sih_deaths_to_sim` runs (hospital and day) find 556 of the 617, 514 of them here; 34 found here only. Before the second pass the 127 unlinked were 78 with no certificate of the same birth date, sex and residence (64 of them with one of the same birth date, sex and discharge day), 28 with two or more candidates in the year, 21 with the key but no death in the year; the second pass took 58.

**The certificate's cause, held out.** Circulatory share (I00-I99) of the linked deaths by band of days, against 27.8% of all deaths of Alagoas residents: 83.3% (1-7 days, 424 pairs), 67.9% (8-30, 240), 53.2% (31-90, 158), 55.4% (91-180, 121), 43.5% (181-365, 161). The control's local rate in those bands (coincidences over pairs) is 1%, 4%, 25%, 30%, 54%; with it, the share of the true deaths solves to 0.83, 0.70, 0.62, 0.67, 0.62: the fall is the coincidences', and the late band is half coincidence (`p_match` 0.46). A rate over the whole year (16%) hides it.

**Designs measured** (the same cohort, the pass alone; chance is the control's share of the pairs): no window, 1,309 pairs, 523 control (**39.95%**, "death after the admission" 91.1%: the 9% before it are false); window of 7, 30, 90, 180, 365, 730 days: 1.24, 2.33, 7.8, 11.2, 18.1, 27.4% (before the one-death rule); a discharge-day-and-hospital pass first, then the window: the window pass reads 27.9% (184 of 660) and is dropped by the 20% rule; the same pass without the hospital second: 233 of 288 pairs by chance, dropped; with it: 58 pairs, 2 control. The one-death rule: without it the join gave 3,941 rows for the 3,879 persons (a person with two candidate deaths in the year took both); with it 1,169 pairs became 1,046 (before the second pass). SIH race is no key: null for 2,806 of 4,671 admissions, "brown" for 1,710 of the other 1,865 (agreement of linked pairs 26%).

**Other topics, the same spec** (AL, 2018-2022; the runs sit beside the first under `_p<digest>` names): I63 principal 608 persons, 106 deaths in 30 days (17.4%, 3.8% coincidences), 197 in 365 (9.6%); I21-I22 on any diagnosis 3,905 persons, 17.5% in 30 days, 28.8% in 365 (15.9%); A41 principal 4,301 persons, 52.0% in 30 days (0.9%), 61.4% in 365 (7.5%): a lethal cohort is less outnumbered by its namesakes. **SINAN-TOXC** P371 2019-2022 (`sinan_toxc_cases_to_sim`, 12,756 children): 724 pairs, 681 control (94%), not viable: 0.3% of these children die and infants born the same day in the same city outnumber them, so the mechanism runs on a notification system and the engine refuses its pairs.
