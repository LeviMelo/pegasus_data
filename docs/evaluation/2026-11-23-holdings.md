# Holdings: what the home holds against what describes it (2026-11-23)

**Why.** The ABNc studies used almost none of what the home holds; a study can only scope what is described. The map's
store-level check (`unclaimed_stores`) passed the Census 2010 microdata because the commuting products read two of its
variables. `holdings()` (`pegasus-data holdings --check`, the map's `holdings` table, replacing `unclaimed_stores`)
compares, store by store, the variables physically held (a lake partition's schema; a CSV's header, inside archives
too) with those its describer names (a dataset's roles, an intake source's columns, IBGE's tract dictionary).

**Regime.** Local home `pegasus_core_data`, master after 67750b83, 2026-11-23. Headers and schemas only.

**Counted.** 218 stores in 8.6 s; the map rebuilt with the table in 256 s at 1.15 GB peak commit. 29 stores carry a
defect, 957 variables held and undescribed:

| store | undescribed | note |
|---|---|---|
| INEP school census (`INEP.EDBASICA`) | 456 of 490 | 34 declared |
| census tracts | 80 of 1,204 | beyond IBGE's dictionary (keys, areas, age bands) |
| S2ID disaster events | 55 of 79 | damage and impact fields |
| CNEFE addresses, INMET stations, CNES kit, SIGTAP and TUSS tables, mobility, ANAC | all | claimed by a product, no variable described |
| TSE electorate 2022, SNGPC lake folder, ANS TISS itemised and remuneration, ANS TabNet beneficiaries | all | no describer |
| Census 2010 microdata | 117 opaque files | fixed width; the layout is in `Documentacao.zip` |

A column's kept raw value and flag (`X__RAW`, `X__FLAG`) count as described by X.

**Also found.** `concept_index` failed whole when a dataset's latest lake year was held for some states only: SINAN's
`condition_codes` and SIA AM's `dispensed` counted it nationally without downloading and raised, so no study scope
could be priced (a spina bifida scope, PegaSUS). Both now take the latest year the lake holds whole and record a
dataset with none as not in the lake; the scope then passed the index. Its record count is slow: counting SIH stays
nationally chains every year's AIHs (`event_types._chained_first`, over 20 minutes of CPU), open.

**Context candidates.** `context_candidates(years, levels)` lists what a study of places could take as context and is
not yet a declared field, or is: for 2015-2023 at the municipality, 107 SIDRA research-subject groups (2,442 tables), 6
of them read by a declared field, and 23 intake sources placed by municipality or state (0.7 s, from the SIDRA registry
and the declarations). PegaSUS's scope lists each with the declaration's decision (`contexts_considered`).

**Shared catalog connection.** Pricing a scope spent minutes in `lake_coverage`: it opened a fresh read-only catalog
connection per call, whose `memo` died with it, and re-parsed 159,662 declared widths (3.6 s) every time. 19 one-shot
read-only openings now take `CatalogStore.shared` (the process's one connection, whose memo is exact against its own
`data_version`): `lake_coverage` 4-7 s to 0.14-0.24 s after the first call. The label-binding build's per-thread readers
are left as they were.

## Columns PegaSUS could not read as fields (2026-11-23)

`pegasus_core.fields.declared` over the 188 role files, before and after (reasons about a column's own declaration; the other reasons
-- years outside the readings, kind not yet read, places, administrative -- are facts and unchanged):

| reason | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| missing codes not declared | 1,834 | 772 |
| missing codes unknown (`missing_unknown`) | 436 | 383 |
| a number that is not a measurement | 378 | 378 |
| role is a draft | 57 | 50 |

1,122 columns left these reasons. How, in order of size:
- `scripts/propose_missing.py` (ADR-0154's rule: DATASUS's labels, the form first) run over every dataset with lake data and undeclared
  columns, read from the lake as held (no fetch), one year per cover step; INEP's 250 categories by the dictionary's own categories
  (kept in each column's note) against every value the lake holds for 2021-2024 (217 `missing: []`, 18 with `9 - Não informado` or
  `Sem declaração`; 12 hold a code the dictionary lacks and stay undeclared: `IN_EDUC_AMB_*` 9, `TP_*` 0).
- Measures (615 columns): the lake's values read, one year, state-capped at 6 M rows. A column declares `missing: []`
  when none of its values is negative, non-numeric or fill-shaped at the maximum (9, 99.., 88..), citing the range and the sources'
  silence; INEP's quantities declare `['88888']` (the dictionary: a marked extreme value above the form's limit). 39 stay unknown
  with the finding (e.g. SINASC FIL_ABORT 99 on 359,471 records, SINAN NUTEMPO 99 and `-1`, text in SINAN-LTAN, SIA-AB AB_PONTBAR all
  non-numeric); 19 are empty in the year read. SIM-DOFET/DOR PESO take SIM-DO's `domain [1, 8000]` (9999 on 55 and 22 records).
- 115 SISCAN legacy counts (no lake data): `missing: []` from the DEF's I (incremento) line, a count the tabulator sums.
- S2iD's four totals confirmed against the file (equal to the sum of their components in every record where non-zero:
  22,379, 32,057, 35,368, 41,375) and un-drafted; DH_TOTAL does not reproduce (21,679 of 34,686) and stays a draft. SRAG
  RES_IGG/IGM/IGA un-drafted (the 2026 form prints their categories, the records agree).
- Three PNI/SINAN columns the tabulation labels with no not-recorded code and whose values are all codes.

**Left, and why.** 50 drafts: INEP's 36 are held in the lake and empty in 2021-2024 (not collected), MT's four state columns and SRAG's
travel history have no layout or hold an undecoded 0, DH_TOTAL does not sum. The 378 "not a measurement" are year, epidemiological
week, coded age (NU_IDADE_N), clock time and magnitudes whose unit is another column (NUTEMPO, TEMPO_FUMA): codes or
calendar, not quantities. 772 undeclared are mostly datasets with no lake data (legacy SISCAN categories, APAC open data, RESP,
SIH base) where nothing tests a declaration; the 383 unknown are tabulation-only categories (SINAN's `9='Ign/Branco'`, ANS,
PNI doses), where the earlier decision (one source does not declare) stands. A finding for a later measurement: SINAN-DENG
HEMA_MAIOR is 0 on 81% of its filled records in 2006 (a hematocrit of 0 is no value), declared `missing: []` for lack of a
documented code.

**Curation read live under a newer schema (2026-11-23).** Curation is read live, so a schema extension (new keys and their validator in one commit) reached running processes as YAML their imported validator refused: the census intake keys stopped a PegaSUS scope, the `source: records` fields stopped a census landing. `read_yaml` now tells the two apart: when the schema files on disk differ from the ones the process imported (`schema.newer_on_disk`), it reads the last copy of that file it validated and warns; with the schema unchanged, a bad key is refused as before.
