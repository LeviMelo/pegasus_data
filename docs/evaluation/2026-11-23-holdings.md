# Holdings: what the home holds against what describes it (2026-11-23)

**Why.** The ABNc studies used almost none of what the home holds; a study can only scope what is described. The map's
store-level check (`unclaimed_stores`) passed the Census 2010 microdata because the commuting products read two of its
variables. `holdings()` (`pegasus-data holdings --check`, the map's `holdings` table, replacing `unclaimed_stores`)
compares, store by store, the variables physically held (a lake partition's schema; a CSV's header, inside archives
too) with those its describer names (a dataset's roles, an intake source's columns, IBGE's tract dictionary).

**Regime.** Local home `pegasus_core_data`, master after 67750b83, 2026-11-23. Headers and schemas only.

**Counted.** 218 stores in 8.6 s; the map rebuilt with the table in 256 s at 1.15 GB peak commit. 29 stores carry a
defect, 957 variables held and undescribed:

| store | undescribed | note |
|---|---|---|
| INEP school census (`INEP.EDBASICA`) | 456 of 490 | 34 declared |
| census tracts | 80 of 1,204 | beyond IBGE's dictionary (keys, areas, age bands) |
| S2ID disaster events | 55 of 79 | damage and impact fields |
| CNEFE addresses, INMET stations, CNES kit, SIGTAP and TUSS tables, mobility, ANAC | all | claimed by a product, no variable described |
| TSE electorate 2022, SNGPC lake folder, ANS TISS itemised and remuneration, ANS TabNet beneficiaries | all | no describer |
| Census 2010 microdata | 117 opaque files | fixed width; the layout is in `Documentacao.zip` |

A column's kept raw value and flag (`X__RAW`, `X__FLAG`) count as described by X.

**Also found.** `concept_index` failed whole when a dataset's latest lake year was held for some states only: SINAN's
`condition_codes` and SIA AM's `dispensed` counted it nationally without downloading and raised, so no study scope
could be priced (a spina bifida scope, PegaSUS). Both now take the latest year the lake holds whole and record a
dataset with none as not in the lake; the scope then passed the index. Its record count is slow: counting SIH stays
nationally chains every year's AIHs (`event_types._chained_first`, over 20 minutes of CPU), open.

**Context candidates.** `context_candidates(years, levels)` lists what a study of places could take as context and is
not yet a declared field, or is: for 2015-2023 at the municipality, 107 SIDRA research-subject groups (2,442 tables), 6
of them read by a declared field, and 23 intake sources placed by municipality or state (0.7 s, from the SIDRA registry
and the declarations). PegaSUS's scope lists each with the declaration's decision (`contexts_considered`).

**Shared catalog connection.** Pricing a scope spent minutes in `lake_coverage`: it opened a fresh read-only catalog
connection per call, whose `memo` died with it, and re-parsed 159,662 declared widths (3.6 s) every time. 19 one-shot
read-only openings now take `CatalogStore.shared` (the process's one connection, whose memo is exact against its own
`data_version`): `lake_coverage` 4-7 s to 0.14-0.24 s after the first call. The label-binding build's per-thread readers
are left as they were.
