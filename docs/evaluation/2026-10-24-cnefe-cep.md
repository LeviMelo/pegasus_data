# CNEFE 2022 landed, checked against IBGE's count by CEP, and Alagoas 2023's AIHs placed (2026-10-24)

**Check.** `pegasus-data cnefe` (ADR-0204) lands the 27 UF zips and derives one row per CEP and municipality. What was counted, and why it
is the thing that matters: (1) per UF, the records written against the CSV's own newlines and its size, since a lost line is a lost address;
(2) per CEP, the addresses, the eight species totals and the four dwelling types against IBGE's own `Agregados_por_CEP`, an independent
figure from the same Census, since a wrong key or a double count shows there; (3) the medians, radii, tracts and levels recomputed apart
(pandas and numpy on the raw records); (4) the coordinates against IBGE's full-resolution municipal mesh and against places whose
coordinates are known; (5) SIH-RD Alagoas 2023's CEPs looked up, since the table exists to place them.

**Regime.** Branch `worktree-agent-a74f705d5cef799e1` from `751c987`, the module as run on 2026-10-24; the one data home
(`pegasus_core_data`, C: with 15 to 32 GB free during the run); the national build detached (`pegasus-data cnefe --json`, log in
`work/cnefe_logs/`); the scenario `scripts/live.py cnefe_cep --home <the home>`, record `data/probes/live/cnefe-national-1/cnefe_cep.json`
(Alagoas with all 27 UFs built; `cnefe-al-2`: Alagoas alone built; `cnefe-fresh-1`: a fresh home, which fetched the AL zip and SIH-RD
from the servers in 72 s, and every figure below equalled the data home's; copies in `registries/ibge_cnefe/probes/`). The manifest
`registries/ibge_cnefe/manifest.json` holds each zip's size, sha256, validators and counts and the check.

**Landing.** 27 of 27 UFs: rows written = the CSV's lines = the per-CEP table's records, no empty `COD_UNICO_ENDERECO`, `COD_MUNICIPIO`,
`COD_SETOR`, `CEP`, `LATITUDE`, `LONGITUDE`, `NV_GEO_COORD` or `COD_ESPECIE`, no `CEP` that is not eight digits, no address whose records
disagree. The detached run landed 23 UFs in 43 minutes (AL, RR, AC and AP came from the slices run first; over the 27, conversion took 526 s
and derivation 281 s, the rest was download); five zips (RS, MS, MT, GO, DF) were deleted once landed because the disk fell under 15 GB free
(the others, 3.3 GB, are kept).

Two readings were wrong first and are why the check is a check. `TOT_END` is the *distinct address*: the records equal it in only 640,355
CEPs (Alagoas 4,323 of 8,417). `TOT_TIPO_104` counts the *dwellings of species 1 and 2*: read on species 1 alone it differed in 338
Alagoas CEPs by exactly the 1,042 collective households (all type 104). A third was a tool's, not IBGE's: DuckDB's `count_if` returns NULL
when every predicate in a group is NULL, so a CEP with no typed dwelling showed neither equal nor different (154 Alagoas CEPs); every
count is now `count(*) FILTER (...)`. `TOT_DOM_COND` has no counterpart among the records and is not compared. A subset check (RR, AC, AP)
had one CEP differ in every column by +4,651 addresses: it spans into a UF not in the subset, and the whole-country check closes it.

**Positions and spread, recomputed apart.** Ten Alagoas CEPs (eight drawn at random, 57160-000 which is its municipality's only
CEP with 33,229 addresses, and Maceió's 57010-000): median latitude and longitude, 50% and 90% radius, tracts with their counts and the six level counts equal pandas and numpy on the
raw records (largest difference 1.4e-14 m). Of Alagoas's 1,561,633 addresses, 1,560,454 (99.925%) lie inside their own municipality's
polygon (mesh `maxima`; 1,118 inside another, 61 in none); 8,419 of 8,422 CEP medians lie inside their municipality. Places with known coordinates: MASP's CEP 01310-200 (33 addresses) has its median 278 m from the museum
(its half-radius 132 m), the Maracanã's 20271-150 (221) 215 m, the Palácio do Planalto's 70150-900 (2) 124 m. Not every CEP is a place:
01001-000, "Praça da Sé", holds 6,973 addresses, all in São Paulo, on 189 street names ("SEM DENOMINACAO" first), half within 17.8 km of
its median, 8.1 km from the Praça; the Congresso's 70160-900 is not in the CNEFE (nor the aggregate).
