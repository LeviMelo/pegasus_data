- **The Distrito Federal.** SISAGUA codes the DF's administrative regions as places of their own (530000-530015:
  Plano Piloto, Ceilândia, ...), and SNGPC names them (Taguatinga, Ceilândia, ...). None is an IBGE municipality, so
  81,596 surveillance rows were unallocated. The DF is one municipality (Brasília, 530010), so a place inside a state
  of one municipality is that municipality. The raw code and name are kept. Re-landed: 0 unallocated of 34,195,729.
- **Names.** Hyphens and apostrophes are punctuation, not part of a name (SNGPC's BIRITIBA-MIRIM is IBGE's Biritiba
  Mirim). Açu/Assú (RN) and Isabel/Izabel (PA) are spelling variants, so they stay unplaced with their names kept.
- **Shares.** A share's population is a field of its own (`outcome` beside `where`; `<name>_assessed`, named as
  `over`). Samples with E. coli present are counted over the samples assessed for E. coli, never over every analysis.
- **The SNGPC dictionary.** The declared URL was a 404. ANVISA's dictionaries are the sales panels' (`/dados/
  Dicionario_de_Dados_Painel_SNGPC_Venda_de_{Industrializados,Manipulados}.pdf`), and their codes are now declared.
  - Compounded sales record a diagnosis on 223 of 3,455,252 lines in 2021.
  - Sex and age are filled on 4.06 M of 11.76 M lines (antimicrobials only).
  - So the B58 evidence must come from the industrialised sales.
- **Industrialised sales 2021.** 57,087,497 rows, equal to the files' lines. A diagnosis is entered on 221,273 lines
  (0.39%). Oral sulfadiazine sold on 311 lines, one with B58; spiramycin on 7,894, none with B58; pyrimethamine on 8.
  Retail sales do not record toxoplasmosis treatment by its diagnosis: the medicines' evidence for the study is SIA's
  APACs and BNAFAR's SUS stock (`2026-10-10-medicines.md`), not SNGPC.
