# Census fields: sanitation, literacy and population by race (2010, 2022)

**Date:** 2026-10-03.

**Regime:**
- branch `linkage`;
- `pegasus-data fields --name ...` into a fresh scratch home, with the twelve
  fields added to `curation/fields.yml` (ADR-0127's mechanism);
- `fields.yml` gains `classification`, which picks one category of an IBGE
  classification. A classification left out is IBGE's Total, checked on
  table 9542 by naming one and reading back the rest as Total.

**Why.** Sanitation, literacy and race composition are the place-level
context most health analyses adjust for. The race counts are also the
denominator for race in SIM, SINASC and SIH (ADR-0128 measured SIH's code 04
against the 0.4% asian share).

**Counted:** the national sum of each field against Census 2022's published
totals.

**Checks.**
- **Race sums to the population.** 2022: 203,069,637 of 203,080,756 (the
  11,119 difference is "sem declaração"). 2010: 190,749,191 of 190,755,799.
- **Status markers.** 5 municipalities are `not_available` in 2010, which
  are those created after it. Zeros are IBGE's "-": 623 municipalities with
  no asian residents and 1,830 with no indigenous residents, in either year.
- **5,570 municipalities in every 2022 field.**

**Found on the way.** The asian population fell from 2,084,288 (2010) to
850,130 (2022), measured here. Why is not established here. Either way,
an "amarela" share in health records should be read against the census of
its own decade.
