# ADR-0194: a cohort link is declared once, run per codes, and reports its false-match rate by follow-up horizon

**Date:** 2026-10-14. **Status:** accepted. **Origin:** PegaSUS writes studies of a condition's hospital admissions and needs 30-day and 1-year mortality after admission, which counts the deaths of those discharged alive. `sih_deaths_to_sim` links only admissions that ended in death (MORTE = 1), and the one link that reached other deaths (`sih_toxoplasmosis_admissions_to_sim`) is written for one topic, its codes inside the filter.

**Context.** A link per topic does not generalise, and a key of birth date, sex and residence cannot be asked to find the death of a person over six years of a nation's certificates: for Alagoas I21-I22 2018-2022 the control found 523 of its 1,309 pairs by chance (40%), because a namesake who died in any of those years pairs as well as the patient. A death must be sought where the patient can have died: in the year after the admission.

**Decision.**
- **Parameters.** A spec may declare `params: {codes: {side, role, any}}`: the caller's ICD-10 codes filter that side's `role` by prefix (`any` names the broader role, every diagnosis, for `match="any"`). `engine.bind` puts the codes into the side's filter, and the bound spec is an ordinary spec to every pass, control, validation and to the probabilistic method (`link_probabilistic(spec=)`). `link(name, codes=, match=)` and `pegasus-data link --codes I21,I22` run it. The run is stored under the codes (`RunKey.params`, a suffix `_p<digest>` that existing file names do not carry), so two topics' runs of one spec never share a file. A spec with `params` declares no `person`: it is a cohort's run, no national one, and the persons merge (`entities`, PegaSUS's `persons`) leaves it out.
- **A window as a key.** `explode_days: [admission.start, 365, follow_up.day]` (the end may be a count of days) gives the left record one row per day of the year after the origin, and the pass keys the day of death to it. `outcome: {origin, event}` names the clock's zero and the right-side date: the event's date is carried into the stored pairs and its last day into the report (`carried`), so a reader needs neither table again. The record-level rule `one_each` applies to a cohort spec: a person who finds two deaths in the window finds neither (a window explodes a record into unique rows, so the unique-key rule alone gave 62 of 3,879 admissions two deaths).
- **The false-match rate is the window's.** The report carries, for 7, 30, 90, 180 days and the window, the pairs, the control's coincidences within that horizon and the verdict that horizon would have alone (`horizons`; `judge`, the one verdict rule). `p_match` of a pair is one minus the control's rate in the band of days it falls in. `linked_deaths` refuses a horizon whose verdict is not viable (`LinkNotViable`) and says where the follow-up ends (`death_data_end`), so a 1-year rate is null for a person followed less.
- **`sih_admissions_to_sim`** (first use), `sinan_toxc_cases_to_sim` (second, SINAN; measured not viable). The cohort is one record per person (birth date, sex, residence), the first admission of the period: the index admission a 30-day and 1-year follow-up counts from, so that readmissions and transfers are not second persons. The right side is SIM-DO of the years of the admissions and the next. Passes: the window pass, then a pass on the discharge day and hospital that takes the in-hospital deaths the first left to a residence the files write differently or a namesake in the year (as `sih_deaths_to_sim` links them). `validate` gains `among` (the pairs a condition selects): the certificate of an in-hospital death says hospital and the hospital's municipality (held out, 99.6% and 99.5%).
- **API.** `pegasus_data.linked_deaths(dataset, codes, years=, geography=)` returns a `Followup`: one row per person (`record`, `origin`, `death_date`, `days_to_death`, `death_record`, `link_pass`, `p_match`, the roles asked for), the quality, the run's pairs and report, `died_within(days)` and `mortality(days)`. It computes the run when absent (`compute=False` raises `RunNotStored`, as PegaSUS's `LinkNotStored`); `link_draws(fu.pairs, report=fu.report)` reads the same pairs.

**Evidence** (`docs/evaluation/2026-10-14-admission-death-link.md`): Alagoas I21-I22 2018-2022, 3,879 persons; the window cut the control from 40% of pairs to 16% inside a year and 2.1% inside 30 days; the certificate's cause (circulatory 83% of the deaths in the first week, 44% of those in days 181-365, 28% of all deaths) falls as the control's local rate rises; 88.8% of the 617 in-hospital deaths are found.

**Not done.** The horizon is the spec's (365); a longer one is another spec or a parameter. The probabilistic method has no block for these specs. Race is not a key (60% of Alagoas's admissions have none, the rest are nearly all "parda"). The 1-year rate is "use with caution", not viable: the 181-365 band is 54% coincidences.

**What would overturn it.** A key that identifies a person across the two systems beyond birth date, sex and residence (the SIH's CNS number, a name) would make the window a convenience, not the condition of a viable link.
