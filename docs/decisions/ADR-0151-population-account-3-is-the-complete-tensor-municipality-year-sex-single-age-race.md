## ADR-0151: population-account-3 is the complete tensor, municipality × year × sex × single age × race

**Date:** 2026-10-05. **Status:** active (stage 1 of 3, `population-account-3`: municipalities, ages, race; stage 2, `population-account-4`, adds the forecast to 2030; stage 3, `population-account-5`, adds 1991-1999; `population-account-6`, item 7, changes the timing of the interior). **Extends** ADR-0148 (the band totals are unchanged). **Request:** the author's verdict that `population-account-2` had basic gaps (11 municipalities missing, no municipal level before 2010, five-year bands, no race).

**Context.** `population-account-2` was place × sex × five-year band, with comparable areas before 2010 and composite entities for the five municipalities created after 2010 (a `+`-joined label that no municipal lookup finds: the 15 municipalities of those entities had no row). Race is not fixed: the Parda share rose from 38.5% (2000) to 45.3% (2022).

**Decision.** Product `population_account` version `population-account-3` (`population_tensor.py`): 5,570 municipalities × 2000-2023 × sex × age 0..100 × race (`total` and five), with 80% and 95% intervals and `basis` (`census`, `intercensal`, `backcast`, `forecast`).
1. **Bands unchanged.** Every band total is population-account-2's (max relative difference 1e-7); single ages 0-79 are the account's own path, 80+ is split into 80-99 and 100+ by the state's census shape.
2. **Municipal split** of a comparable area (2000-2009) or composite (2000-2023, anchored on 2022): each member's census count at the earliest census that counts every member, the 2007 Contagem where every member has one, moved back by half the member's 2010-2022 growth differential. Registered births by residence as the indicator of a member's share were tested against a constant share on 3,583 pairs of neighbouring municipalities and did worse (0.19 against 0.12 at ten years); the census growth differential at weight 0.5 did best (0.099), and with the Contagem as anchor 0.086. The error law (σ² = σ₀² + k·years, by size class) is that of the pairs; on the 56 real creations it covers the Contagem 2007 at 0.82 (80%) and 0.93 (95%).
3. **Race** by cohort composition: log shares are linear in time along each birth cohort between censuses (2000 sample by band, 2010 and 2022 universe by single age), a cohort born after a census is moved back from the next by the state's per-year *reclassification rate* of the previous decade, and after 2022 forward by the state's drift. Identification: a state's cohort composition change is reclassification, differential migration and mortality together; the model assigns it all to reclassification (small beside it at the state level) and carries the municipal deviation as spread. Rates are capped at 0.08 a year (the Asian count of 2010 was 2.1M, of 2022 0.85M: an instrument change, not a trend). Shares are smoothed over ±2 ages with a Dirichlet prior from the state.
4. **Intervals.** The band's interval of ADR-0148 in quadrature with the split's, the single age's share inside its band (a² + b²·u, u the multinomial term; scaled by the bridge factor of the year) and the race share's (same form, one parameter pair each for bridge, back and forward paths); all measured out of sample (evaluation entry).

5. **Forecast (stage 2, version `population-account-4`, a version is never overwritten).** Births and deaths are projected from their national trends and the account is moved by the migration regression at the horizon, to 2030, with the account's interval at the horizon plus 0.003 a year for the projection of events; validated by pseudo-forecasts from 2000 and 2010 (evaluation entry): calibrated to eight years from both bases and twelve from 2010.

6. **Backcast (stage 3, version `population-account-5` = `-4` plus 1991-1999, `race = total` only).** Units are the comparable areas 1991-2010 (about a thousand municipalities were created in the decade). The vital account is run **backward** from the coverage-corrected Census 2000 with SIM deaths only (no birth: SINASC starts in 1994, incomplete) and settled on the Census 1991 (SIDRA 200, five-year groups), the departure carried along each cohort; 1991 coverage is the 2000 census's by state × sex × band carried back (an assumption). Intervals are the cross-period spread of ADR-0148 times 1.2 with the bridge between the two censuses; a municipality created after 1991 is split from the 2000 estimate and the Contagem 1996 where every member was counted. Hold-out on the Contagem 1996 (never used by the area estimate): median area error 0.028 after the national rescale, 80% / 95% coverage 0.83 / 0.94 (evaluation entry, Backcast). Basis is `backcast` (`census` for 1991 where the municipality is its own area). The level at 1996 is 5.0% above the Contagem's raw count and not validated; race is not estimated (the 1991 race by municipality is a total, by age only for states); before 1991 is not built.

7. **Timing of the residual (`population-account-6` = `-5` with a changed interior).** The census-to-census residual of each cohort was carried through the years evenly; it is net migration (net of coverage change), and the cohort residuals of 2000-2010 and 2010-2022 sit at ages 15-27 in the same shape in both decades (small places lose 3-4% of a cohort a year at 18-22). `distribute_residual` and the 1991 `settle` now give each cohort the age schedule's rate (`fit_schedule`: yearly rates by age, per sex and size class, second-difference penalty, averaged over the two periods) times its size and spread only the rest evenly. Censuses, band totals at 1991/2000/2010/2022 and the 2023-2030 forecast are unchanged; interior years differ (women 20-44 of places under 20,000 by 1-2% in 2013-2020). The Contagem 1996 by age (out of period) shows the even timing biased by 2% at ages 15-29 of small areas, removed; area totals at 1996 and 2007 are unchanged (evaluation entry, Timing of the residual). Interval spreads of the interior are those of the even path (not recalibrated).

**Not decided / not calibrated.**
- Race shares at the census years are smoothed, not the raw census cell; the race interval at a census is the account's own.
- The five post-2010 creations are anchored on 2022 alone with a constant share (births did not help); their 2000-2010 backcast is 12-22 years from the anchor, beyond the pairs' reach in the other direction, and a fast grower (Mojuí dos Campos) is probably under-covered.
- Single-age intervals inside a band are calibrated on the end of a period and bridged; ages above 80 are the state's shape, not the municipality's.
- The 1990s' level (coverage of the 1991 census), 1991 race, and anything before 1991; the forecast's 100-500k municipalities (80% coverage 0.64 at twelve years from 2010) and its single-age and race spreads (ten- and twelve-year hold-outs scaled by horizon).
- `fields.py` and `sus_dependent.py` are pinned to `population-account-2` (band-level intervals).
