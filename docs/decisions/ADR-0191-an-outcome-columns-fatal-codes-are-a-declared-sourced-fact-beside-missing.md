# ADR-0191: an outcome column's fatal codes are a declared, sourced fact beside `missing`

**Date:** 2026-10-14. **Status:** accepted. **Origin:** PegaSUS computes case fatality (in-hospital mortality from SIH, "letalidade" from SINAN) and had only the Portuguese label of an outcome code to read it from.

**Context.** Which code of an outcome column means death is not one number. SINAN's `EVOLUCAO` is `2` (death by the notified disease) and `3` (death from another cause) in 30 forms (dengue, chikungunya, Zika, leptospirosis, Aids, meningitis ...), `3` and `4` in six (diphtheria, schistosomiasis, exogenous intoxication, both leishmaniases, poliomyelitis) and in tuberculosis' `SITUA_ENCE`, `5` and `6` in two (the biological-exposure accident, the severe work accident), `6` and `7` in seven (cancer and the work-related diseases); a yes/no flag (SIH `MORTE`, APAC `AP_OBITO`) is `1`. A consumer that matches "óbito pelo agravo" at run time depends on spellings that differ by form and year (`Óbito por LV`, `óbito por TNN`), and one that hard-codes `2` is wrong for 16 of the 46 forms. The case fatality counts the deaths of the notified disease only, and a code "Óbito em investigação" (dengue, chikungunya, violence) or a fetal loss (congenital syphilis' `aborto`, `natimorto`) is no death of that case.

**Decision.**
- A column of `curation/roles/*.yml` may declare `fatal`, the codes that mean death by the event's own cause (the notified disease; a yes/no flag's "yes"), and `fatal_other`, the codes of death from another cause. They sit beside `missing`, are carried on `ColumnRole` and in `roles()` / `pegasus-data roles --json` as `fatal` and `fatal_other` (a list, or `None` when undeclared), and are checked on load: a non-empty list of strings, each once, on a `category`, `fatal_other` only with `fatal`, no code in both lists or in `missing`.
- Each is written from DATASUS's labels for that column and the comment beside it names the table and the labels (`# from DATASUS's labels (the form's own dictionary, CURATED.SINAN.DENG.EVOLUCAO; 2007-2026): 2='óbito pelo agravo', 3='óbito por outras causas'; 4='óbito em investigação' is neither`). A form's own dictionary decides; without one the table that labels the records does (seven forms, read on their records). A code that is neither is in neither list and the comment says so.
- A column no label decodes stays undeclared: SINAN's 2000-2006 `CON_EVOLUC` (CHAG, DENG, HANT, LEIV, LEPT, LTAN, MALA: the Windows layout has no published dictionary) and HIVE's `EVOLUCAO` (empty: 11 of 6,088 records filled in 2015, none in 2017, 2020 or 2024).
- Declared: 73 columns. `fatal` and `fatal_other` on the outcome column of 46 SINAN datasets (`case.outcome`, and TUBE's `treatment.outcome`); `fatal` alone on `MORTE` of SIH-RD, SIHSUS-RJ, CIHA and CIH-CR (`admission.death`), `AP_OBITO` of the ten SIASUS and ten DADOS_ABERTOS APAC layouts (`authorisation.death`), `PA_OBITO` of SIASUS-PA, and `ST_OBITO` of RESP-RESP and RESP-RESPBR.

**Not done.** The denominator of a case fatality is the consumer's: it is the confirmed cases with a known outcome (leptospirosis 2022: all 322 fatal records are confirmed, 11.0% of the 2,920 confirmed with a known outcome, 2.1% of all 15,217 notifications), and no role here says which rows are confirmed. `treatment.exit_type` (HANS `TPALTA_N`) and SIH's `COBRANCA` (`admission.discharge_reason`) may carry death codes too; their roles are not outcomes of the notified event and were not examined.

**What would overturn it.** A form whose outcome codes change meaning inside the years of one declaration (the comment names the years of the labels it rests on); the declaration would then need windows, as a curated code has (ADR-0189).
