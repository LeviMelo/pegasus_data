# OPEN_QUESTIONS.md

Unresolved empirical questions and known gaps. **Append here rather than
inventing an answer silently.** When one is resolved, the decision goes to
`DECISIONS.md` as an ADR, or the measurement to `EVALUATION.md`, and the row
moves to `docs/history/open_questions_resolved.md`, whole and annotated.

Status: `open` · `measuring` · `resolved -> <where>`

Consolidated on 2026-09-28 from the August 2026 record, de-duplicated:
`docs/history/pegasus_data_ARCHITECTURE.md` §20 and §22,
`docs/history/FINDINGS.md` §4 and V8, `docs/history/AGGREGATE_PLAN.md` §8–§9,
`docs/history/AGGREGATE_ALGEBRA.md` §7 and §10, `docs/history/HANDOFF.md` §4,
`docs/history/RESUME.md` (known open items) and
`docs/history/IBGE_LOCALIDADES.md` §6. Rows checked against `src/` on
2026-09-28 where a grep could settle it; the check is named in the row.

This table is not the catalog's own ledger. The catalog records a
per-column question for every code-bearing column bound to no codelist (1,339
at the last count, ARCH §20); `pegasus-data questions` lists them. A row here
is a question about the project, not about one column.

**Where to look.** Meaning and decoding: 1–8, 21, 25–28. Geography: 9–11.
The aggregate layer: 12–16. Identifiers: 17–19. Evidence and scale: 20,
22–24.
