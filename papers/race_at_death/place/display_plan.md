# Display plan - paper 1, place

Claims in order, one main item each, with the sentence a reader should be able to say after it.

| # | Claim | Item | The reader says |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The same infant on two records, a census reference and the place | Figure 1, `flow_infants` (design, with counts) | "296,512 infant deaths were linked to their birth records; the census gives how households classify other children." |
| 2 | The certificate differs from both the mother and the family | Figure 2, `classifications` | "Certificates give the mother's race to 0.76 of White, 0.64 of Brown and 0.18 of Black mothers' infants, and depart from the family in the Black row most." |
| 3 | The certificate varies by place far more than the family does, in clusters | Figure 3, `map_municipal` | "Brown mothers' infants certified White range from 0.09 to 0.76 across municipalities, clustered, much more than families' classification." |

| 5 | Over time the categories barely move; within places composition moves the certificate much less than between them | Figure 5, `place_time` (A years, B map of change, C yearly panel with six covariates, D census designs with fixed and following benchmarks) | "Inside a place composition moves the certificate little in the yearly panel and the census decade; the long panel does not discriminate; the verification-service share moves it within places." |
