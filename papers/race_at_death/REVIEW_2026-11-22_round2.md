The first review's points were worked, and the two tests it asked for changed what the papers say. What is left is
mostly of one kind: **new results were added to the papers without rewriting the parts they overturn.** A paper
whose finding changed needs its title, abstract, first Discussion paragraph and conclusion rewritten from the new

## 2. Paper by paper

### Paper 1, place

1.1 **The Methods were cut too far.** "Analyses" is now one sentence with eleven citations and a pointer to a
supplementary text, and Results points to "Supplementary Text S4" seven times. A reader must be able to tell from the
main text what each model in Results is: its outcome, family, units, random effects and the covariate blocks. Restore
one or two sentences per analysis that Results reports (smoothing, Moran, binomial deviance models, border test,
multilevel and crossed models, the panel, the two new tests). About 300 words.

1.2 **"Understated" needs its argument.** The abstract concludes that, because the birth record leans toward the
local majority, the certificate's dependence on place "is understated". That follows only through a selection
argument (in whiter places the mothers still recorded Brown are the less ambiguous ones, and the reverse in darker
places). State the argument in the Discussion in two sentences and word the abstract as what the result implies under
it. Also name the rival readings of a slope above 1 against the census: fertility differing by race along
composition, the census respondent answering for the mother, and the two years' gap.

1.3 The objective and the conclusion still say "infants and children"; the title now says infants. Use one.

1.4 Table 1 is the best single item of the set. Keep it in the main text whatever the journal's limits.

### Paper 2, indicators

2.1 **A stale sentence contradicts the new method.** "Two classifications in each term" still says linked deaths
were "scaled to all certificates by certified race, since the linked share differs by race". Methods, Results and the
table note say post-stratification. Find every description of the scaling and make them one.

2.2 **The anchor is for the wrong ratio.** The usual computation reproduces a published Indigenous to White ratio.
The paper's claim is about White to Brown. Say what published sources report for White against Brown infant
mortality from these systems, reproduce one if any exists, and if none does, say that plainly: then "the usual way"
is the way the systems invite, and the paper's contribution is to show what it would give.

2.3 **The misclassification correction is unreadable** ("its off-diagonal cells from reversibility, one declaration
taken as its square root"). Give the idea in one plain sentence in the text and the algebra in the supplement.

2.4 Paper 3 now reports that in some states the birth record counts about half the Black mothers the census holds
(5.2% against 10.9%). Those mothers are in another class of this paper's denominators and numerators. Add it to the
limitations with its direction, and give the Black to Brown ratio for the states where the birth record's Black share
matches the census.

### Paper 3, Black category

3.1 **Rewrite from the four levels, do not append them.** The ladder figure is rebuilt and good. The text is not:
- the abstract's Results give the four levels and then repeat 0.18 and 0.36 with their intervals a second time;
- the first Results section is still "Three records of the same families" and opens with "Each later record used the
  Black category less than the mothers had declared it";
- Figure 2's note still says "The declaration bar is 1 by construction", which describes the old figure;
- the Discussion opens with the old comparison (hospital 0.37 against certificate 0.19 for the same infants) and the
  paragraph on the right comparators is the fourth of six;
- the conclusion still ends "fewer than the hospitals recorded their mothers", the mother-against-infant comparison
  the review asked to drop.
Rebuild the abstract, the first Results section, the Discussion's first paragraph and the conclusion around: the
mother repeats her own declaration in 0.52; families report her infant Black in 0.39; the hospital records her in
0.36; the certificate records the infant in 0.18.

3.2 **Is the 0.52 the mother or the hospital?** The paper shows that the admission record repeats in 0.92 in the
same hospital and 0.58 in another. Do the same for the birth record's declaration: among mothers with two births,
the share declaring Black again when the second birth was in the same establishment and when it was in another. If
it is high in the same establishment and low in another, the "declaration" is largely the establishment's, and the
reference of all six papers is weaker than assumed. If it is alike, the instability is the mother's. Either result
belongs in the abstract.

3.3 "Where" is two sentences with state abbreviations. Write the state names in full and say what the map shows
beyond the extremes.

3.4 The abstract is about 450 words. Four findings, four sentences.

### Paper 4, family

4.1 "Much alike across the country" sits beside "by state from 0.06 to 0.17", a threefold range. Say what it is
small against (the certificate's gradient) and do not call it alike without that.

4.2 The pull toward White and away from Black is the paper's most general finding and is the fourth clause of the
conclusion. Lead with it.

4.3 The abstract runs past 400 words.

### Paper 5, violence

5.1 **The finding is no longer about violence.** Assaults change exactly as transport deaths do (-0.6 points); the
contrast is external against natural causes; and it halves among deaths in hospital. The title, the keywords
(Homicide, Violence), the Introduction's framing around homicide and the Implications still argue a paper about the
race of homicide victims. Rewrite them for what was found: on these certificates, deaths certified away from a
hospital and a family are recorded White less often than the same people were recorded before, whatever the
external cause.

5.2 **Finish the decomposition the new section starts.** Natural deaths occur in hospital in 82% of cases and
assaults in 17%, and within each cause the change is larger outside hospital. Standardise the external causes to the
place-of-death distribution of natural deaths (or fit cause and place of death together) and report how much of the
11.5 points remains. "Part goes with the place of death, and part remains" is not a result. Then put the informant
reading (a family present against a body examined) among the readings of the Discussion, where the paragraph "Who
certified did not explain the difference" still stands unchanged.

5.3 The Discussion's "three readings" paragraph predates the place-of-death result; add the reading and its test.

### Paper 6, estimator

6.1 The abstract and the stated domain (about one chance candidate per unit; validated at every age for hospital
deaths) are now right.

6.2 The 2011 paper is still unread. Two human-side routes remain and are the author's: opening the Statistics Canada
page in an ordinary browser and saving the PDF into the paper's folder, or a library copy. Until then the text must
say the method is described from the 2015 paper by the same first author, which it now does; keep that sentence.

## 3. Across the papers

**A. Propagate the weak reference.** Paper 3's finding that the birth record's race is partly the hospital's
(5.2% against 10.9% Black in some states) and paper 1's finding that it leans toward the local majority are results
about the reference every paper uses. Each paper's limitations should cite both with the direction of their effect
on that paper's main number. Papers 2 and 5 do not yet.

**B. Abstracts.** Papers 2, 3 and 4 exceed 400 words. Whatever the journal, no structured abstract will be allowed
more than about 300. Cut them now to 300 with the four main findings; the build already counts words.

**C. One rule for rewrites.** When a result changes a paper's claim, list the places that state the claim (title,
abstract objective and conclusion, last paragraph of the Introduction, first paragraph of the Discussion, conclusion,
figure and table titles, keywords) and rewrite each. Add to each paper's build an assert that the conclusion sentence
of the abstract and of the text agree.
