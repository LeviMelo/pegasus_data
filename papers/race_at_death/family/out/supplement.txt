Supplementary material - The race of a child in the household: partners, schooling, age and who answers, in the Brazilian Censuses of 2010 and 2022
Supplementary Text S1 - The cohort series
The share of each five-year birth cohort reported White at the Censuses of 2000, 2010 and 2022 was read from a cohort-component population account of every municipality, built for this research programme from the censuses, the mortality and live birth registers and the official estimates of IBGE; in a census year the account takes the census population by single year of age and race as given, so the shares at the three dates are census shares. A cohort's White share can fall between censuses through reclassification, but also through higher mortality or emigration of White people, differences in coverage between censuses and the change of the 2022 questionnaire; the series describes the cohorts and does not separate these.
Supplementary Text S2 - Design-based standard errors
For a weighted proportion r = sum(w y) / sum(w), the linearised variable z = w (y - r) / sum(w) was summed within households and its variance estimated as the between-household variance within states, sum over states of n_h / (n_h - 1) sum (z_hi - mean_h)^2, which treats households as sampled with replacement within states. With the weighting areas of 2010 as strata the standard error of Brown mothers' children aged 0-9 reported White was 0.00050, against 0.00050 with states. The logistic models use the weights scaled to a mean of 1 and standard errors clustered by household.
